## Example 1: Twitter Thread → Analysis Essay

### Input (Chinese Twitter Thread)

```
1/ 大家都在恐慌AI替代整个行业。CBRE -12%，JLL -12%，CH Robinson -15% — 
都因为一个前卡拉OK公司发布了AI物流工具。

2/ 但我作为真正在做AI产品的人看到的是：市场恐慌不是关于"AI会不会替代我"，
而是关于定价权。上游有定价权的（NVDA，能源基础设施）没事。
下游服务公司（CRM，物流）被锤了。

3/ 我一直在自己的产品决策中观察这个现象。我的Skill平台连接企业数据。
我不断问自己：我的产品能给客户定价权，还是只帮他们降低成本？

4/ zvec一天获得+1,094个GitHub stars。嵌入式向量搜索，本地运行。
对我的日本B2B客户来说意义重大，数据驻留不可谈判。

5/ 我的建议：不要跟随恐慌，跟随定价权。如果你的AI产品能帮客户提价
（而不只是省钱），你就站在了这次重新定价的正确一边。
```

### Output (Substack Essay)

```markdown
# AI Panic Is a Pricing Power Test

*Everyone's panicking about AI replacing entire industries. But what if we're misreading the signal?*

---

Last week, a Chinese tech company — one that used to run a karaoke app — shipped 
an AI logistics tool. Within days, CBRE dropped 12%. JLL dropped 12%. CH Robinson 
dropped 15%.

The takes wrote themselves: *AI is coming for your job. No one is safe.* 

I don't buy it. Not because AI isn't disruptive — it very much is — but because 
the panic is aimed at the wrong thing. This isn't a story about job replacement. 
It's a story about pricing power. And the distinction matters more than most 
people realize.

## The real repricing

Here's what the market is actually telling us.

Upstream players with pricing power — NVIDIA, energy infrastructure, the 
picks-and-shovels crowd — are holding up fine. Downstream service companies — 
CRMs, logistics brokers, the people who sit between buyer and seller — are 
getting crushed.

This isn't a cycle. It's a permanent repricing of the entire value chain.

The market isn't saying "AI will take these jobs." It's saying "AI will erode 
these margins." That's a very different claim, and it has very different 
implications for what you should build next.

## The question I keep coming back to

I've been watching this play out in my own product decisions. I run a platform 
that connects AI tools to enterprise data — think of it as an intelligence 
layer for B2B sales teams. Every week, I'm making bets about what to build 
and what to skip.

The question that keeps nagging at me: *Does my product help customers charge 
more, or does it just help them spend less?*

Cost-cutting tools become commodities. If your AI product saves a customer 
$10,000 a month, that's great — until three competitors ship the same thing 
and the savings converge to zero. You end up in a race to the bottom, 
competing on price for a product that destroys price.

Pricing power tools survive. If your AI product helps a customer identify a 
$2M deal they would have missed, or helps them position against a competitor 
in a way that justifies a premium — that's durable. That's something they'll 
pay for even when budgets tighten.

## What I'm building toward

Don't follow the panic. Follow the pricing power.

If your AI product helps customers charge more — not just spend less — you're 
on the right side of this repricing. Everything else is a commodity waiting 
to happen.

---

*If this resonated, consider sharing it with someone navigating the same 
questions. And if you have a different take on the pricing power thesis, 
I genuinely want to hear it — drop a comment below.*
```

### Key Transformations Applied

1. **Thread numbers removed** — replaced with narrative flow
2. **Each tweet expanded** — from one sentence to 1-3 paragraphs with reasoning
3. **"前卡拉OK公司"** → "a Chinese tech company — one that used to run a karaoke app" (context added naturally)
4. **"食物链"** → "value chain" (correct business English term)
5. **Personal experience woven in** — "I run a platform..." instead of "My Skill platform..."
6. **Subtitle added** — Substack convention for email preview
7. **CTA added** — engagement-driving closing

## Example 2: Phrase-Level Transformations

These show common Chinese → Native English conversions at the sentence level:

| Chinese Pattern | ❌ Translated English | ✅ Native Substack English |
|----------------|----------------------|---------------------------|
| 众所周知 | As we all know | [just state the fact directly] |
| 值得注意的是 | It is worth noting that | [state it directly — if it's worth noting, just note it] |
| 越来越多的人 | More and more people | Increasingly, people... / The shift is accelerating: |
| 起着重要作用 | plays an important role | shapes / drives / defines |
| 一方面...另一方面 | On one hand... on the other hand | [develop contrast naturally without signposting] |
| 随着...的发展 | With the development of... | As X evolves, ... / X has changed the math on... |
| 不可否认 | It is undeniable that | [just assert it — if it's undeniable, your readers will agree] |
| 综上所述 | In summary / To sum up | [write a genuine closing thought, not a summary label] |
| 我认为 | I think / I believe | I've come to see... / The way I read this... / Here's my take: |
| 具有重要意义 | has important significance | matters because... / changes the equation for... |
